4. Nútíð og þátíð

Mundu!
  • Nútíð með he, she, it (líka Madison, Zaheer, school …): bættu -s við. I play – he plays.
  • have verður has með he, she, it: Jessica has a twin brother.
  • I am · you, we, they are · he, she, it is.
  • Þátíð (yesterday, last week): bættu -ed við. play → played, like → liked.
  • Óreglulegar sagnir lærir maður utanbókar: be → was/were, have → had, do → did, go → went, take → took, come → came.
